Each method has two separate horizontal bars starting at zero: a light gray dotted bar for train-only time and a family-colored patterned bar for exposed tail. They are not stacked. Both bars use the same post-warmup epochs and repeat selection. In iteration units, both are means per measured optimizer step, not median iteration latency. Shared horizontal limits support comparison across panels. The baseline is omitted when tail instrumentation is unavailable.
